Who We Are

A school built for Golarchi's future

New City Public High School was founded with a clear purpose: to give the children of Golarchi access to the same quality of education found in the region's larger cities, without their families having to leave home to find it.

Over the years, we have grown from a small primary section into a complete institution offering education from Montessori through Matriculation, while holding on to the same values we started with — discipline, care, and academic honesty.

Our Foundation

Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality education that builds strong academic foundations, sound character, and the confidence students need to pursue their goals — within a safe and supportive campus.

Vision

To be recognised as the leading educational institution in Golarchi and the wider Badin district, known for producing disciplined, capable, and community-minded graduates.

Our Journey

Milestones

  • 1998

    School Founded

    New City Public High School opens its doors in Golarchi with a single primary section.

  • 2006

    Middle Section Introduced

    Classes extended up to Class 8 as student enrolment grew across the district.

  • 2013

    Matriculation Program Launched

    The school became a full high school, offering Science and General groups through Class 10.

  • 2024

    Campus Expansion

    New classrooms, a science laboratory, and a computer lab added to serve a growing student body.

How It Works

Admission process

01

Submit Inquiry

Fill out the admission inquiry form below or visit the school office directly.

02

Assessment & Interview

Students are assessed at their applied grade level; parents meet with school administration.

03

Confirm Enrollment

Complete document submission and fee payment to secure the seat for the session.

Requirements

Documents needed

  • Form B

    Original and one photocopy of the child's Form B / birth certificate.

  • Photos

    Four recent passport-size photographs of the student.

  • Report Card

    Previous school's leaving certificate and last report card, where applicable.

  • Parent CNIC

    Photocopy of both parents' or guardian's CNIC.
